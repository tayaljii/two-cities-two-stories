Two Cities. Two Stories — Roorkee & Joshimath
==============================================
Interactive HTML presentation deck

HOW TO RUN
1. Unzip this archive anywhere (keep present.html and the assets folder together).
2. Double-click present.html — it opens in any modern browser
   (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari).

PRESENTING
- Arrow keys (← →) or on-screen buttons: navigate slides
- N: toggle the built-in speaker notes (script, extra context,
   transitions and timing for each slide)
- E: edit any text on a slide live (for last-minute fixes)

NOTES
- The deck is 16 slides, 1920×1080. Press F (or the browser's fullscreen
  button) for a clean full-screen presentation.
- Fonts (Fraunces, IBM Plex) load from Google Fonts on first open;
  with no internet the deck still works with system fonts.
- Slide 14 is a reference/credits slide — do not present it.
- Slide 15 is the "Any Questions?" slide — pause here and take questions.
- Slide 16 is the closing film. Click the title card once to play it full
  screen with sound (it is a local video file, so no internet is needed).
  When it ends, a closing card appears; click again to replay. Moving to
  another slide stops and rewinds it automatically.
