# Sources & Image Credits — "Two Cities. Two Stories."

Companion document for the presentation *Two Cities. Two Stories: Exploring the Heritage, Culture and Identity of Roorkee & Joshimath* (Om Tayal & Saloni).

## 1. Factual sources

### Roorkee
| Claim used in deck | Source |
|---|---|
| Roorkee listed in the *Ain-i-Akbari* (Mughal administrative record, 1590s); 18th-century Landhaura estate under Raja Ramdayal; pre-1840 village of mud huts; canal digging began April 1842; canal opened 1854 irrigating ~767,000 acres | Nagar Nigam Roorkee, "About Roorkee" — https://nnroorkee.uk.gov.in/about-roorkee/ |
| Roorkee College founded 1847; renamed Thomason College of Civil Engineering 1854; University of Roorkee 1949 (India's first engineering university); IIT Roorkee 2001 | IIT Roorkee — Institute History — https://www.iitr.ac.in/Institute/History.html ; Heritage pages — https://www.iitr.ac.in/Institute/Heritage.html |
| Solani Aqueduct: canal bed ~25 m above the river; canal carried ~0.5 km over the Solani; opened 1854 | Institution of Civil Engineers — https://www.ice.org.uk/what-is-civil-engineering/infrastructure-projects/the-ganges-canal ; Ganges Canal (Wikipedia summary of primary reports) |
| 22 Dec 1851 — India's first steam freight run, Roorkee → Piran Kaliyar, hauling earth for canal works ('Jenny' locomotive; also referenced as 'Thomason'); ~2 years before India's first passenger train | Indian Railways historical accounts & contemporary records (multiple corroborating sources, e.g. railways-of-raj compilation; Indian Railways fan-wiki citations) |
| Bengal Sappers (Bengal Engineer Group) based at Roorkee; among India's oldest cantonments | Nagar Nigam Roorkee; Bengal Sappers institutional history |
| Piran Kaliyar Sharif — dargah of Hazrat Alauddin Ali Ahmed Sabir (13th c.); ~25 km from Roorkee; Haridwar/Har Ki Pauri ~30 km | Uttarakhand Tourism — https://uptourism.gov.in ; Nagar Nigam Roorkee |

**Evidence hygiene.** Traditional/heritage age figures (temple ~1,200 years, Kalpavriksha) are presented as tradition, not established archaeology. The hydropower project's role in subsidence is presented as **disputed**, not as established fact. The 2023 figure of ~868 damaged houses is labelled with its date (Feb 2023) and not presented as current status.
